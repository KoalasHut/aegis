# Procedure

1. Restate the concrete need and identify assumptions.
2. Challenge alternatives and consequences at the domain level.
3. Separate approved intent from proposals and mandatory unknowns.
4. Ask the user for decisions needed for consolidation.
5. Return the brief; hand off only after required approvals.

Close with one outcome under the stage contract. Approval and assignment amendments are recorded by their issuer/dispatcher, not manufactured by this role.
