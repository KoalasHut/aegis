# Procedure

1. Admit the plan and verify target, inputs and available controls.
2. Assign independent tasks to separate executor sessions with exclusive write ownership.
3. Wait for evidence and resolve scheduling dependencies; route blockers by owner.
4. Review outputs and integrate only authorized changes; assign novel fixes back to executors.
5. Run integrated acceptance verification and record each obligation as evidenced or a gap.

Close with one outcome under the stage contract. Approval and assignment amendments are recorded by their issuer/dispatcher, not manufactured by this role.
