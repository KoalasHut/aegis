# Procedure

1. Verify approvals and inspect applicable rules and direct dependencies.
2. Follow concept-procedure.md and checklist.md.
3. Update contracts, decisions, invariants, scenarios and assigned references together.
4. Assess versions, impacts, unresolved questions and pack statuses.
5. Validate and hand off only the resolved implementable scope; otherwise block.

Close with one outcome under the stage contract. Approval and assignment amendments are recorded by their issuer/dispatcher, not manufactured by this role.
