# Illustrative core-preservation decision narrative

`decisions.yaml` is the authoritative structured decision log for this isolated
example. This Markdown file is explanatory narrative only. Its records do not
represent a project's approval, authority, or decision history.

## D-CORE-001 — Identify every approved rule

An approved domain rule receives a stable identifier so scenarios and blocks can
refer to it.

## D-CORE-002 — Keep policy choices explicit

Policies identify their accountable owner and approving decision so they can be
revisited deliberately.

## D-CORE-003 — Express calculations as visible decisions

Calculations state their inputs, results, and cases where those choices matter.

## D-CORE-004 — Retain externally imposed constraints

Constraints keep their external source so the reason for the limit survives a
rewrite.

## D-CORE-005 — Record business quality expectations

Quality expectations remain business language rather than implementation terms.
